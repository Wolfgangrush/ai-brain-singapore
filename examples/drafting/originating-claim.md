## When to use an Originating Claim (OC)

Use an OC where there is likely to be a **substantial dispute of fact** (Order 6, Rule 1, Rules of Court 2021). The OC replaced the "Writ of Summons" from 1 April 2022 and is served together with (or followed by, within 14 days) the Statement of Claim — see [`statement-of-claim.md`](./statement-of-claim.md).

## Procedural anchors

- **Validity for service:** 3 months from date of issue.
- **NIC (Notice of Intention to Contest) by defendant:** 14 days (in Singapore) / 21 days (outside).
- **Defence:** within 21 days of service of the Statement of Claim — see [`defence.md`](./defence.md).
- **Reply:** within 14 days of Defence — see [`reply.md`](./reply.md).
- **Form-number anchor:** Verify the prescribed Form number for OC in the current ROC 2021 Subsidiary Legislation Annex / Practice Direction for the operative court (Supreme Court / State Courts) before filing. Research-note pointer: `_research/36-court-form-originating-claim.md`.

## Discipline checklist before issuing

- [ ] **Cause of action**: every cause is pleaded with the necessary elements; limitation period verified against [`s06-limitation-act.md`](../../_statute_corpus/s06-limitation-act.md).
- [ ] **Court of competent jurisdiction**: subject-matter and monetary thresholds (Supreme Court / State Courts / Family Justice Courts) verified.
- [ ] **Party particulars**: NRIC / Passport / UEN cross-checked against authoritative records.
- [ ] **Service address**: a valid Singapore address for service is stated.
- [ ] **Statement of Claim**: drafted, comprehensive on material facts and reliefs — pleadings amendments later require leave and cost penalty (Order 9 ROC 2021).
- [ ] **Filing fees**: confirmed against the current Schedule of Fees on judiciary.gov.sg.
- [ ] **AI-use disclosure (if applicable)**: comply with the Judiciary's *Guide on the Use of Generative AI Tools by Court Users* (October 2024).
- [ ] **Counsel sign-off**: reviewed by a Singapore-admitted advocate and solicitor before filing.

## Verbatim anchor (ROC 2021)
