# Affidavit — Singapore (ROC 2021)

PROVENANCE: CITED:_research/02-court-rules-civil.md · _research/13-citation-format-primary.md · _research/37-court-form-originating-application.md

> **Use of this template:** Scaffold for qualified Singapore counsel. Replace every `<PLACEHOLDER>` with verified case-specific data. An affidavit is sworn / affirmed evidence — the deponent's contents must be within the deponent's knowledge (or sources of information / belief expressly identified).

---

## When affidavits are required

- Supporting an **Originating Application** (Order 6 / 15, ROC 2021) — see [`originating-application.md`](./originating-application.md).
- **Affidavit of Evidence-in-Chief (AEIC)** for civil trials (Order 15 ROC 2021) — direct evidence-in-chief is reduced to writing.
- Interlocutory applications (summary judgment, injunctions, security for costs, etc.).
- Probate / family / mental-capacity proceedings — affidavits of due execution, of assets, etc.

## Drafting principles

- **First-person, in the deponent's own words** — but counsel-drafted is the practical norm.
- **Personal knowledge unless otherwise stated** — for information / belief from another source, state the source and basis of belief.
- **One paragraph per fact** — numbered consecutively.
- **Exhibits** identified by capital letters, paginated, and referred to by paragraph (e.g., "exhibited herewith and marked 'A-1' is a copy of …").
- **No legal argument** — affidavits state facts, not submissions.
- **No hearsay unless permissible** — apply [`s05-evidence-act.md`](../../_statute_corpus/s05-evidence-act.md).

## Exhibit / pagination discipline

- Each exhibit bears a unique reference (typically `<DEPONENT INITIALS>-N`).
- Exhibits paginated sequentially.
- Cover sheet on each exhibit: "This is the document marked '<REF>' referred to in the affidavit of <DEPONENT NAME> affirmed / sworn on <DATE> before me." — signed by the Commissioner for Oaths.

## Discipline checklist

- [ ] **First-person voice** maintained throughout.
- [ ] **Source of information / belief** stated wherever facts are not within personal knowledge.
- [ ] **No legal submission** smuggled in — facts only.
- [ ] **No hearsay unless permissible** (cross-check [`s05-evidence-act.md`](../../_statute_corpus/s05-evidence-act.md)).
- [ ] **Exhibits paginated, indexed, and properly endorsed**.
- [ ] **Commissioner for Oaths / Notary Public / authorised officer** with current authority.
- [ ] **Identification confirmed** — NRIC / Passport sighted before affirmation.
- [ ] **AI-use disclosure (if applicable)** — comply with Judiciary's *Guide on the Use of Generative AI Tools by Court Users* (October 2024).
