## Discipline notes

- **Form numbers** in the Rules of Court 2021 are prescribed in the Subsidiary Legislation Annex. The numbers vary by court (Supreme Court / State Courts / Family Justice Courts). Every template includes a "Form-number anchor" line that should be replaced with the actual form number after a fresh check against the relevant Court's current Practice Directions on judiciary.gov.sg. Where this scaffold and the v0.1 research notes disagree, the SSO / Practice Directions govern — and the discrepancy is flagged in `singapore/BLOCKERS.md`.
- **Citation style:** all citations follow the Singapore Academy of Law Style Guide (2021). Use "v" (not "vs"). Italicise party names. Neutral citations: `[Year] SGCA NN` (Court of Appeal) · `[Year] SGHC NN` (General Division of the High Court) · `[Year] SGCA(I) NN` (Court of Appeal, Civil) etc. — verify the suffix against the latest SAL guide.
- **Counsel review required:** every output produced from these templates must be reviewed by an advocate and solicitor admitted to practice in Singapore before filing. The templates are scaffolds — the operative pleading is always the advocate's.
- **AI-generated content:** if any portion of the draft is AI-generated, comply with the Judiciary's *Guide on the Use of Generative AI Tools by Court Users* (October 2024) — including any required acknowledgement / verification step.
