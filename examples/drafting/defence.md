# Defence — Singapore (ROC 2021)

> **Use of this template:** Scaffold for qualified Singapore counsel. Replace every `<PLACEHOLDER>` with verified case-specific data. The Defence is the Defendant's responsive pleading to the Statement of Claim.

---

## When to use a Defence

The Defence must be filed within **21 days** of service of the Statement of Claim (Order 9, ROC 2021). It must address every material allegation in the Statement of Claim — admitted, denied, or not admitted (the Defendant putting the Claimant to strict proof).

## Drafting principles

- **Address each paragraph of the SOC** in order — admit, deny, or not admit.
- **Non-admission ≠ denial.** Use **denial** when actively disputing a fact; **non-admission** when calling for proof without affirmatively asserting the contrary.
- **Plead positive case** — defences are not limited to denials; affirmative defences (e.g., set-off, counterclaim, limitation, contributory negligence) must be expressly pleaded.
- **Particulars where required** — fraud, misrepresentation, negligence, breach particulars demand specificity.
- **Counterclaim**, if any, is pleaded in the same document under a separate heading.

## Discipline checklist

- [ ] **21-day deadline** computed from service date; diary entered.
- [ ] **Every SOC paragraph addressed** — bare denial of a fact pleaded against the Defendant may be deemed admission.
- [ ] **Affirmative defences** identified and pleaded (limitation / contributory negligence / set-off / waiver / estoppel / illegality / consent / volenti / public policy).
- [ ] **Counterclaim** — if any, decided early; affects discovery and costs.
- [ ] **Particulars** of affirmative defences with the same specificity as the SOC.
- [ ] **Counsel sign-off** before filing.
