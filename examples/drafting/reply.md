## When to file a Reply

A Reply must be filed within **14 days** of service of the Defence (Order 9, ROC 2021). The Reply is **not** an opportunity to repeat the SOC — it is confined to:
- joining issue on new matters raised by the Defence;
- responding to affirmative defences (e.g., limitation / contributory negligence / set-off);
- responding to a counterclaim (this part is a **Defence to Counterclaim**, conventionally combined with the Reply).

## Drafting principles

- **Narrow scope** — do not re-plead the SOC.
- **Implied joinder** — failure to file a Reply implies joinder of issue on the Defence; an active Reply is needed where new affirmative defences or counterclaims must be addressed.
- **No new cause of action** — new causes require amendment of the SOC under Order 9 ROC 2021 (leave required).

## Template

```
IN THE <COURT>

ORIGINATING CLAIM NO. <NO> OF <YEAR>

BETWEEN

    <CLAIMANT FULL NAME>     ... Claimant

                                    AND

    <DEFENDANT FULL NAME>    ... Defendant


REPLY <AND DEFENCE TO COUNTERCLAIM>


REPLY

1.  Save in so far as the same consists of admissions, the Claimant joins issue with the Defendant on his / her Defence.

2.  As to paragraph 11 of the Defence (limitation):
    (a) The Claimant denies that the cause of action accrued on or before <DATE> as alleged.
    (b) The Claimant pleads that the cause of action accrued on <DATE>, being the date on which <ACCRUAL FACT>.
    (c) Further or alternatively, the Claimant relies on <s 24A LA 1959 (date of knowledge) | s 29 LA 1959 (fraud / concealment) | other postponement provision>.

3.  As to paragraph 12 of the Defence (contributory negligence):
    (a) The Claimant denies any contributory negligence;
    (b) The Claimant pleads that the loss was caused wholly by the Defendant's <breach | negligence>;
    (c) <PARTICULARS, IF NEEDED>.

4.  As to paragraph 13 of the Defence (set-off):
    (a) The Claimant denies that any sum is due from him / her to the Defendant;
    (b) <PARTICULARS>.


DEFENCE TO COUNTERCLAIM (if a counterclaim has been pleaded)

5.  The Claimant adopts and repeats paragraphs 1–4 above as if set out here.

6.  As to paragraph 16 of the Counterclaim, the Claimant <admits | denies | does not admit> <PARTICULARS>.

7.  <CONTINUE PARAGRAPH-BY-PARAGRAPH RESPONSE TO THE COUNTERCLAIM, INCLUDING ANY AFFIRMATIVE DEFENCE TO THE COUNTERCLAIM.>

AND THE CLAIMANT PRAYS:

(1) That the Defendant's counterclaim be dismissed;
(2) Costs of the counterclaim; AND
(3) Such further or other relief as this Honourable Court deems fit.


Dated this <DATE>.

___________________________________
<COUNSEL NAME>
<LAW PRACTICE NAME>
Solicitors for the Claimant
(Ref: <FILE REF>)
```

## Discipline checklist

- [ ] **14-day deadline** computed from service of the Defence; diary entered.
- [ ] **Scope discipline** — no new causes of action; no re-pleading of the SOC.
- [ ] **Affirmative defences responded to** — limitation / contributory negligence / set-off / illegality / waiver as raised.
- [ ] **Defence to Counterclaim**, if applicable, addresses every counterclaim paragraph.
- [ ] **Counsel sign-off** before filing.
