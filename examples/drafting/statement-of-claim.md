# Statement of Claim — Singapore (ROC 2021)

PROVENANCE: CITED:_research/02-court-rules-civil.md · _research/36-court-form-originating-claim.md · _research/13-citation-format-primary.md

> **Use of this template:** Scaffold for qualified Singapore counsel. Replace every `<PLACEHOLDER>` with verified case-specific data. The Statement of Claim ("SOC") is the operative pleading that defines the claim — it must be comprehensive on the material facts and the reliefs sought.

---

## When to use a Statement of Claim

The SOC is filed together with the Originating Claim (OC) or served within 14 days of the OC (see [`originating-claim.md`](./originating-claim.md)). It sets out the material facts upon which the Claimant relies and the reliefs sought.

Under ROC 2021, parties are expected to "put their cards on the table" early. Subsequent amendments require leave of court and frequently attract cost penalties — draft to be comprehensive on the first pass.

## Drafting principles

- **Material facts only** — do not plead evidence; plead the facts that constitute each element of each cause of action.
- **One paragraph per material fact** — numbered consecutively.
- **Express every cause of action** — pleaded with each element identified.
- **Reliefs precisely framed** — courts can only grant what is pleaded.
- **Costs and interest** — always pleaded as separate prayers.

## Template

```
IN THE <SUPREME COURT OF THE REPUBLIC OF SINGAPORE | STATE COURTS OF THE REPUBLIC OF SINGAPORE>
<GENERAL DIVISION OF THE HIGH COURT | DISTRICT COURT | MAGISTRATE'S COURT>

ORIGINATING CLAIM NO. <NO> OF <YEAR>

BETWEEN

    <CLAIMANT FULL NAME>     ... Claimant

                                    AND

    <DEFENDANT FULL NAME>    ... Defendant


STATEMENT OF CLAIM


PARTIES

1.  The Claimant is <DESCRIPTION OF CLAIMANT — natural person / Singapore-incorporated company / etc., and capacity in which the claim is brought>.

2.  The Defendant is <DESCRIPTION OF DEFENDANT>.


MATERIAL FACTS

3.  On <DATE>, <MATERIAL FACT 1>.

4.  On <DATE>, <MATERIAL FACT 2>.

5.  <CONTINUE PLEADING MATERIAL FACTS IN CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER. ONE FACT PER PARAGRAPH.>


CAUSE OF ACTION 1 — <e.g., BREACH OF CONTRACT>

6.  By an agreement <in writing | partly in writing and partly oral | wholly oral> dated <DATE> and made between the Claimant and the Defendant ("the Agreement"), <MATERIAL TERMS>.

7.  The express / implied terms of the Agreement included that <TERMS>.

8.  In breach of the Agreement, the Defendant <PARTICULARS OF BREACH>:

    Particulars of Breach
    (a) <PARTICULAR>;
    (b) <PARTICULAR>;
    (c) <PARTICULAR>.

9.  By reason of the matters aforesaid, the Claimant has suffered loss and damage.

    Particulars of Loss and Damage
    (a) <ITEM> — S$<AMOUNT>;
    (b) <ITEM> — S$<AMOUNT>;
    (c) <ITEM> — S$<AMOUNT>;
    Total: S$<TOTAL AMOUNT>.


CAUSE OF ACTION 2 — <FURTHER CAUSE OF ACTION, IF ANY (e.g., NEGLIGENCE / MISREPRESENTATION)>

10. <REPEAT THE ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT PLEADING STRUCTURE FOR EACH ADDITIONAL CAUSE OF ACTION.>


AND THE CLAIMANT CLAIMS:

(1) Damages assessed at S$<AMOUNT> or such other sum as this Honourable Court shall find;
(2) Interest on damages pursuant to <section 12 Civil Law Act 1909 | other statutory basis>;
(3) Costs of and incidental to this action; AND
(4) Such further or other relief as this Honourable Court deems fit.


Dated this <DATE>.

___________________________________
<COUNSEL NAME>
<LAW PRACTICE NAME>
Solicitors for the Claimant
(Ref: <FILE REF>)
```

## Discipline checklist

- [ ] **Element-by-element**: each cause of action's elements verified pleaded.
- [ ] **Time-bar check** against [`s06-limitation-act.md`](../../_statute_corpus/s06-limitation-act.md).
- [ ] **Particulars** — fraud / misrepresentation / breach particulars stated with sufficient specificity to satisfy Order 6 / Order 9 ROC 2021.
- [ ] **Reliefs framed precisely** — court grants what is pleaded.
- [ ] **Interest basis stated** — statutory (s 12 Civil Law Act 1909) or contractual.
- [ ] **Costs prayer included**.
- [ ] **Counsel sign-off** before filing.
