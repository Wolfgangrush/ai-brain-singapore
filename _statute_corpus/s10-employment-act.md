- **Regulator:** Ministry of Manpower (MOM) — https://www.mom.gov.sg

## Topic Digest

### A. Coverage
The Employment Act applies to every employee under a contract of service with an employer, with limited exclusions:
- Seafarers (governed by the Merchant Shipping Act).
- Domestic workers (separate MOM framework).
- Most public servants (separate framework).

Foreign employees are within scope where they hold a valid work pass and have a contract of service.

### B. Core Provisions (All Covered Employees)
- **Annual leave:** minimum 7 days in the first 12 months, rising with length of service (capped at 14 days).
- **Sick leave:** paid sick leave (outpatient + hospitalisation) accrues with service.
- **Public holidays:** paid public holidays.
- **Salary payment timing:** at least monthly, and within 7 days after the end of the salary period.

### C. Part IV Protections (Specific Groups Only)
Special protections — rest days, maximum hours of work, overtime — apply only to:
- **Workmen** (manual / equivalent) earning **up to S$4,500/month** in basic salary; and
- **Non-workmen** (junior office staff) earning **up to S$2,600/month** in basic salary.
(Thresholds as last published — verify current figures at MOM.)

### D. Wrongful Dismissal Claims
A dismissal must be with "just cause or excuse." Wrongful-dismissal disputes are primarily routed through:
- **Mandatory TADM mediation** (Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management); and
- **Employment Claims Tribunal (ECT)**, with statutory monetary caps.

### E. Contract of Service vs. Contract for Service
Critical classification:
- **Contract of service** — employer / employee — Act applies.
- **Contract for service** — principal / independent contractor — Act generally does NOT apply.
The classification turns on the multi-factor test (control, integration, economic reality, mutuality of obligation).

### F. Retrenchment
There is **no statutory entitlement** to retrenchment benefits. MOM publishes Tripartite Advisories (and the Fair Consideration Framework — FCF) setting expected practice. Contractual retrenchment provisions in the employment contract govern.

### G. Salary Payment Discipline
Section 21 / Pt III timing rules — salary paid at least once a month and within 7 days of the end of the salary period. Default-on-salary triggers MOM enforcement and is a frequent dispute trigger.

## Verbatim Anchor Quote
> "Every employee shall be entitled to paid annual leave … of not less than 7 days for the first 12 months of continuous service with the same employer."
> — Section 43, Employment Act 1968.

## Citation Format
- **Section reference:** s 43 EA 1968 / s 14 EA 1968 (termination).
- **Long form:** Employment Act 1968 (Singapore).

## Cross-statute Relationships
- **Employment Claims Act 2016** — establishes the ECT.
- **Work Injury Compensation Act 2019** — separate workplace-injury regime (WICA replaces WICA 2008).
- **PDPA 2012** — employee personal data is within PDPA scope (see [s01-pdpa-data-protection.md](./s01-pdpa-data-protection.md)).
- **Personal Data Protection Commission Advisory Guidelines on Employment** — regulator-issued cross-walk.
- **Contract law** — termination, restraint of trade, and notice-period clauses interact with the EA (see [s02-contract-law-overview.md](./s02-contract-law-overview.md)).

## Practical Implications for a Solo Advocate
- **Classification audit** at intake: verify whether an "independent contractor" is in substance an employee. Mis-classification triggers retrospective EA liability + tax exposure.
- **Part IV thresholds** are the most common surprise — middle-management staff above the non-workmen threshold are NOT entitled to overtime under the EA; contractual overtime is then the only path.
- **Termination clauses** must satisfy both EA notice minima and any contract-of-service stipulations; non-compete clauses must additionally pass common-law restraint-of-trade scrutiny.
