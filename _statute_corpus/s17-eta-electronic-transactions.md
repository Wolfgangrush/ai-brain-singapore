# Statute Digest: Electronic Transactions Act 2010 (ETA) — Singapore

- **Common short form:** ETA
- **Official URL:** https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/ETA2010
- **Regulator:** Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA) — https://www.imda.gov.sg

- **Tier:** Tier 1 (Mandatory).

- **Original Enactment:** 2010.

## Language
- **Language:** English (Authoritative).

## Scope of Digest
- **Topics covered:** Legal recognition of electronic records (s 6); electronic signatures (s 8); secure electronic signatures (Pt 5); Schedule 1 exclusions; contract formation by electronic means; integrity of electronic records (s 9).
- **Topics deferred:** Specific certification-authority regime (Pt 7 / Pt 8 procedural detail) — pointer only.

## Topic Digest

### A. Legal Recognition — Section 6
> "For the avoidance of doubt, it is declared that information shall not be denied legal effect … solely on the ground that it is in the form of an electronic record."

This is the foundational equivalence provision — an electronic record is **not invalid merely because it is electronic**.

### B. Electronic Signatures — Section 8
An electronic signature is valid where:
- a **method** is used to identify the person and to indicate their **intention** in respect of the information contained in the electronic record; AND
- the method is, in light of all the circumstances (including any agreement between parties), **as reliable as appropriate** for the purpose for which the record was generated.

### C. Secure Electronic Signatures (Part 5)
Where a signature meets specified additional criteria (e.g., uniquely linked to the signatory; capable of identifying the signatory; created using means under the signatory's sole control; linked to the record in a way that detects subsequent alteration), it is a **secure electronic signature** carrying **higher legal presumptions** of authenticity and integrity.

### D. Schedule 1 — Exclusions
The ETA's electronic-signature recognition is **NOT** available for:
- **Wills** (subject to the strict Wills Act 1838 formalities — see [s08-probate-administration.md](./s08-probate-administration.md)).
- **Negotiable instruments** (subject to the 2021 amendments allowing prescribed electronic execution under specified conditions).
- **Indentures and deeds** for the sale or transfer of immovable property (conveyancing instruments — see [s12-property-land-law.md](./s12-property-land-law.md)).
- **Powers of Attorney** (with limited exception for Lasting Powers of Attorney executed via the OPG's dedicated electronic platform — see [s14-mental-capacity-act.md](./s14-mental-capacity-act.md)).

### E. Contract Formation (Part 4)
Offer and acceptance can be expressed by means of electronic records. Confirms that **electronic contract formation is valid** subject to ordinary contract-law principles (formation; consideration; intention; capacity) — see [s02-contract-law-overview.md](./s02-contract-law-overview.md).

### F. Integrity of Electronic Records — Section 9
Where a rule of law requires information to be presented or retained in its **original form**, the requirement is met by an electronic record if there is **reliable assurance** as to its integrity from the time it was first generated, and the record is **capable of being displayed** to the recipient.

## Cross-statute Relationships
- **Contract Law** — formation, terms, breach (see [s02-contract-law-overview.md](./s02-contract-law-overview.md)).
- **Evidence Act 1893** — admissibility of electronic records as evidence; integrity and authentication intersect with s 9 ETA (see [s05-evidence-act.md](./s05-evidence-act.md)).
- **Wills Act 1838** — wet-ink + two-witnesses formalities are NOT displaced by the ETA (see [s08-probate-administration.md](./s08-probate-administration.md)).
- **Land Titles Act 1993 / CLPA** — conveyancing instruments remain outside ETA recognition for electronic execution (see [s12-property-land-law.md](./s12-property-land-law.md)).
- **Mental Capacity Act 2008** — LPA-specific electronic-execution platform (OPG) operates within the ETA exclusion regime (see [s14-mental-capacity-act.md](./s14-mental-capacity-act.md)).

## Practical Implications for a Solo Advocate
- **Engagement letters / general commercial contracts:** electronic signatures (e.g., DocuSign, HelloSign) are generally valid; document the platform's identification + intention features in the file note.
- **Wills:** **never** sign electronically. Wet-ink + two witnesses present at the same time. No exception.
- **Real estate Options to Purchase / Sale & Purchase Agreements:** treat as wet-ink. Confirm with the SLA / conveyancer before innovating.
- **LPAs:** use the OPG's dedicated electronic-execution system — do NOT default to a generic e-signature platform.
- **Negotiable instruments:** check the 2021-amendment specifics carefully before electronic-execution reliance.
