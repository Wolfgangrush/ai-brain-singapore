## Topic Digest

### A. Types of Companies
- **Private Limited Company (Pte Ltd)** — most common; up to 50 shareholders; shares cannot be offered to the public; the default vehicle for an SME or a law-corporation form for a solo practice.
- **Public Limited Company** — can offer shares publicly.
- **Unlimited Company** — members have unlimited liability (rare).

### B. "Small Company" Audit Exemption
A private company is "small" — and thus exempt from statutory audit — if, for each of the preceding two financial years, it meets at least **two of three** criteria:
- (a) Revenue not exceeding S$10 million;
- (b) Total assets not exceeding S$10 million;
- (c) Number of employees not exceeding 50.

### C. Directors' Duties — Section 157
Directors must act honestly and use reasonable diligence in the discharge of the duties of their office. Section 157(1) is the statutory anchor; the common-law fiduciary duties (act in good faith in the company's interests, for proper purposes, avoid conflicts) apply concurrently.

### D. Register of Registrable Controllers (RORC)
Companies must maintain a **non-public** register of individuals who exercise significant control over the company, and lodge equivalent data with ACRA's Central RORC. "Significant control" includes >25% share / voting interest, or the right to appoint / remove a majority of directors.

### E. Annual General Meetings (AGMs)
Section 175A allows a **private company** to dispense with holding AGMs if (a) all members have, by resolution, dispensed with AGMs; or (b) the company sends financial statements to members within 5 months after the financial year-end; or (c) the company is a dormant relevant company exempt from preparing financial statements.

### F. Share Capital
- **No minimum share capital** to incorporate (S$1 is common).
- Shares may be ordinary, preference, or redeemable (subject to articles).
- Capital reductions require either court approval or a solvency-statement procedure under s 78B / 78C.

### G. Statutory Secretary
Every company must appoint a qualified company secretary **within 6 months** of incorporation. For a private company, the sole director may not also act as secretary (s 171(1AA)).

## Verbatim Anchor Quote
> "A director shall at all times act honestly and use reasonable diligence in the discharge of the duties of his or her office."
> — Section 157(1), Companies Act 1967.

## Practical Implications for a Solo Advocate
- A solo advocate operating a **Singapore Law Corporation** (one form of practice under LPA Part 4A — see [s07-legal-profession-act.md](./s07-legal-profession-act.md)) wears two regulatory hats: solicitor (LPA + PCR 2015) AND director (Companies Act). Director duties under s 157 apply concurrently with professional-conduct duties.
- SME clients: surface RORC compliance, the small-company audit exemption, and the s 175A AGM-waiver mechanism early in the engagement.
- Director-disqualification risk: directors of a company that has entered insolvent liquidation may face disqualification under IRDA s 149 / 150 — cross-check before accepting new directorships.
