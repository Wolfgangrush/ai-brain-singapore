## Topic Digest

### A. Admission to the Bar (Part 2)
Sets the qualifying requirements to be admitted as an "advocate and solicitor" of the Supreme Court. Pathways include the Singapore Bar Examinations / Part B (post-2013) and the Practice Training Period (the supervised period in practice).

### B. Practising Certificate (Part 3)
- A lawyer wishing to practise law in Singapore must hold a **current Practising Certificate (PC)** issued by the Registrar of the Supreme Court.
- Annual renewal cycle (1 April to 31 March).
- PCs may be issued subject to conditions (e.g., supervision conditions for early-year practitioners).

### C. Privileges of Advocates and Solicitors (Part 4)
- **Right of audience** in the courts of Singapore.
- **Reserved acts** (Section 33) — only advocates and solicitors (and certain authorised persons) may practise as such or do "any act as an advocate and solicitor." This is the principal UPL surface.
- **Conveyancing acts** — drawing documents transferring real property is largely reserved.

### D. Professional Conduct and Discipline (Part 6)
- The **Law Society** receives complaints; **Inquiry Committee** → **Disciplinary Tribunal (DT)** → **Court of 3 Judges** is the principal escalation path.
- Substantive conduct rules are contained in the **Legal Profession (Professional Conduct) Rules 2015 (PCR 2015)** — referenced by the LPA.
- Sanctions range from written warning, fine, suspension, to striking off the roll.

### E. Remuneration (Part 8)
- Governs fee agreements, taxation of costs (by the Registrar), and prohibitions on certain fee structures.
- **Contingency fee agreements (no win no fee)** are not generally permitted in Singapore contentious work.
- **Conditional Fee Agreements (CFAs)** were introduced for prescribed categories of work (e.g., certain arbitration and international commercial matters) — verify current scope at PCR / LPA subsidiary legislation level before reliance.

### F. Law Society of Singapore (Part 5)
Defines the role and powers of the Law Society — including representative, regulatory, and disciplinary functions and the Compensation Fund.

### G. LSRA Licensing (Part 9A)
The **Legal Services Regulatory Authority (LSRA)** licenses law practices (Singapore Law Practices, Foreign Law Practices, and Qualifying Foreign Law Practices) and regulates foreign-lawyer registration. Annual licensing + reporting obligations apply.

### H. Forms of Singapore Law Practice
Permitted forms include Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), and Law Corporation. Each has distinct insurance, naming, and accounts implications.

## Verbatim Anchor Quote
> "No person shall practise as an advocate and solicitor or do any act as an advocate and solicitor unless his or her name is on the roll and he or she has in force a practising certificate."
> — Section 33, Legal Profession Act 1966.
